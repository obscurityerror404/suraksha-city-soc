🏛️ 1. Why Smart Cities are Vulnerable: IT vs. OT

Traditional enterprise IT security prioritizes Confidentiality (preventing data leaks). In contrast, Smart City Operational Technology (OT / SCADA) prioritizes Human Life Safety and System Availability.

The Legacy Protocol Trap: Industrial protocols like Modbus (1979) and DNP3 were engineered before the internet existed. They contain zero cryptographic authentication—any device that gains network access can command pumps or trip power breakers.
Reference: ISA/IEC 62443 Security for Industrial Automation

⚡ 2. Anatomy of an Attack: How Breaches Occur

Smart city cyber attacks rarely start with movies-style brute force. They infiltrate through secondary bridges:

  • Physical Roadside Taps: Splicing into roadside traffic cabinets.
  • Third-Party Vendor VPNs: Compromised HVAC or maintenance contractor laptops.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MitM): Injecting false setpoints while falsifying human operator screens.
Dangerous Consequence: Physical overpressure, conflicting green traffic signals, and hospital ICU blackouts.
Mapped to: MITRE ATT&CK for Industrial Control Systems (ICS)

🔍 3. How Modern Systems Detect Attacks

SURAKSHA-CITY employs multi-layered kinetic cyber detection:

  • Deep Packet Inspection (DPI): Flags unauthorized Modbus Function Code 0x10 from non-whitelisted IPs.
  • Physics-Based Telemetry Anomalies: Detects hydraulic pressure derivatives ($dP/dt > 1.6\text{ Bar/s}$) before pipe burst.
  • Hardware Interlock Trips: Voltage monitors trip Conflict Monitor Units (CMU) on traffic faces.
Detection Engine: Dynamic Signature + Physical Sensor Correlation

🛡️ The 5 Golden Rules for Infrastructure SOC Analysts

When alarms trigger in a Municipal Security Operations Center, analysts must execute these 5 non-negotiable steps:

1. DO NOT Power Down Blindly

Abruptly cutting power to a water pump causes massive hydraulic water hammer ruptures. Always engage mechanical fail-safes first.

2. Zero-Trust Network Isolation

Sever the compromised Ethernet / 802.1X switch port remotely to stop malicious packet streams without shutting down the physical plant.

3. Engage Hardware Fail-Safes

Switch traffic signals to Flashing Amber, trigger pneumatic pressure relief valves, and island hospital microgrids.

4. Lock Cryptographic Evidence

Capture PCAP dumps, compute SHA-256 hashes, and archive logs for legal prosecution and CERT-In compliance.

5. Verify Civic Continuity

Confirm that secondary gravity reservoir lines, ambulance corridors, and microgrids keep public services 100% active.
